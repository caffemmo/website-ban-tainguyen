# Caffemmo Domain Glossary

## Proxy accounting

- A proxy batch is one provider purchase recorded in `proxy_ipv6_batches`.
- Procurement cost is the provider cost of a batch or a direct provider order.
- Sales revenue is money collected from non-refunded proxy orders.
- Realized gross profit is sales revenue minus the cost of proxy units already assigned to customers.
- Inventory capital is the provider cost of available or reserved IPv6 units that have not been sold.
- Expected inventory profit is the current retail value of unsold IPv6 units minus their procurement cost.
- Cash difference for a period is sales revenue in that period minus procurement payments recorded in that period. It is not the same as realized gross profit when a batch is purchased before its units are sold.
